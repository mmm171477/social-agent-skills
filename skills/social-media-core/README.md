# social-media-core — the shared brain behind every platform skill

For anyone running more than one social channel: it keeps brand voice, campaigns, calendars and quality checks consistent, and hands the platform-specific work to the right specialist skill.

## What it gives you
- A **brand brief** workflow (voice, audience, content pillars, do and don't) so every post sounds like one company.
- Campaign planning and a content calendar, with one source idea adapted into platform-native versions instead of one post pasted everywhere.
- A QA gate for every item (facts, claims, rights, disclosures, accessibility) and an approval summary before anything is published.
- A learning loop: what to measure, how to read it, and what to change next.

## Try it
- "Build a 4-week launch campaign for these 3 platforms from this one announcement."
- "Fill in the brand brief from our existing posts and flag what is missing."
- "Adapt this article into a YouTube idea, an Instagram carousel and a TikTok script."

## You bring
Your goals, audience, assets and approvals. It will tell you when a task belongs to a platform skill and route it there.

## Boundaries
Strategy and quality control only: it is not a connector or a publishing service. It will not fabricate analytics or help with unsolicited bulk messaging.

Install: see the suite README, or `python scripts/install_skills.py --skill social-media-core`. Install the platform skills you use alongside it.
