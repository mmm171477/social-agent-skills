# Social media core playbook

## 1. Strategy in five questions
1. Who exactly is this for?
2. What do they need, in their words?
3. What do we want them to do next?
4. Why would they trust us on this?
5. How will we know it worked, and by when?
If an answer is unknown, write `unknown` and plan how to find out. Do not fill gaps with plausible guesses.

## 2. Content pillars
Choose three or four recurring themes (for example learn, proof, community, offer). Each post belongs to one pillar. Rebalance monthly from what your audience actually engages with.

## 3. Adapt, do not paste
One source idea, then a native version per platform: different hook, length, framing and call to action. Keep the core message identical; change everything the format demands.

## 4. Calendar discipline
Every item has: platform, format, date and time zone, owner, approver, asset status, claim-check status. An item without an approver does not ship.

## 5. QA gate
Facts and numbers sourced. Claims provable. Rights cleared. Required disclosures present. Accessibility handled (captions, alt text, contrast). Tone matches the brief.

## 6. Measurement
Pick one primary metric per item that matches its goal (reach for discovery, saves for teaching, replies for community, clicks for traffic). State the window and sample size. Compare like with like.

## 7. Learning loop
Weekly: what we shipped, what happened, what we decide. Keep decisions short and dated. Retire what does not work; double down on what does, one variable at a time.

## 8. Routing
Single-platform technical work goes to that platform's skill. Never promise capabilities a platform skill or host tool does not have.
