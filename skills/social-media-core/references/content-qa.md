# Content quality gate

Mark each item PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN and add evidence.

- [ ] Objective and audience are explicit.
- [ ] Product, price, availability, warranty and legal claims are verified.
- [ ] Copy matches approved brand voice and market language.
- [ ] Media rights, model releases, music and trademarks are cleared.
- [ ] No deceptive engagement bait, impersonation or undisclosed sponsorship.
- [ ] Captions, contrast, readable text and accessibility are checked.
- [ ] Link, CTA, tracking and destination are correct.
- [ ] Platform format, size, duration and policy constraints are checked against current docs.
- [ ] Exact account, channel, date, time zone and schedule are shown to approver.
- [ ] Human approval recorded before external side effects.
