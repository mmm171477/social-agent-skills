# snapchat-agent — Public Profile content, with an honest access check first

For brands and creators who publish to Snapchat Public Profiles and want content made for the app, plus clarity on what they are actually allowed to automate.

## What it gives you
- An **access matrix** that separates organic Public Profile publishing from paid advertising APIs, since access to one does not prove access to the other.
- Story and Spotlight creative planning in a casual, in-the-moment style that fits the app.
- A content checklist, caption and asset notes, and a **manual fallback** when no approved integration exists.
- Performance review prompts tied to what the connected product actually exposes.

## Try it
- "Plan a week of Stories for a launch, with one Spotlight idea."
- "Do we qualify to publish through an API? Walk me through the access questions."
- "Our integration is not approved yet. Give me the manual posting checklist."

## You bring
Brand voice, assets, and the facts about your profile eligibility and any approvals you hold.

## Boundaries
Instructions and checklists. No access, credentials or connector included, and no assumption that a developer app has Public Profile permission. Paid-ads workflows are out of scope.

Install: see the suite README, or `python scripts/install_skills.py --skill snapchat-agent`.
