# WhatsApp policy and access verification

Verify current Meta Business Platform terms before implementation, including business/account eligibility, opt-in, template approval and categories, customer-service windows, quality rating, throughput, pricing, regional requirements and permitted message types. Do not encode policy details as permanent assumptions.

Official starting points:
- WhatsApp Cloud API: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/
- Getting started: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/get-started
- WhatsApp Business Platform: https://business.whatsapp.com/
