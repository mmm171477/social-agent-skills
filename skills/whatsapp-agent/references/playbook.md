# WhatsApp Business playbook

## 1. Consent is the gate
Before any outbound message, confirm a recorded opt-in for that purpose. Record who, when, how, and for what. No record means no message. Make opting out as easy as opting in, and honour it immediately.

## 2. Message types
| Type | Example | Extra care |
|---|---|---|
| Transactional | order shipped, appointment reminder | tie to a real event; no marketing mixed in |
| Service | reply to a customer who wrote first | answer from approved facts only |
| Marketing | promotion, announcement | separate opt-in; stricter policy review |
Check the official policy and the current rules for template categories and conversation windows before relying on them.

## 3. Writing a template
One purpose, plain language, variables for the facts, no invented dates or promises, a clear sender, an opt-out path. Keep it short enough to read at a glance.

## 4. Support conversations
Greet, identify the need, answer from the system of record, confirm the result. If the facts are missing, say so and hand off. Never guess a status, a refund or a delivery date.

## 5. Escalation
Hand to a human on: complaints, legal threats, safety issues, payment problems, account-security incidents, a customer asking for a person, or two failed attempts to resolve. Pass a short summary so the customer does not repeat themselves.

## 6. Quality and limits
Respect provider quality ratings and sending limits; never try to evade them. Watch block and report signals as a sign to send less, not more.

## 7. Verification
Treat "sent" and "delivered" as provider facts. After a timeout, reconcile before retrying so a customer does not get the same message twice.

## 8. Never
Message purchased lists, automate a personal account, use unofficial clients, or send marketing without opt-in.
