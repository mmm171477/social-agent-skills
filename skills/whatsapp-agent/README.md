# whatsapp-agent — consent-first customer messaging

For businesses that talk to customers on WhatsApp and need to do it responsibly: with opt-in, approved templates, clear handoffs to people, and no spam.

## What it gives you
- A **consent-first** workflow: no message plan without a recorded opt-in; a consent register template is included.
- Template drafting for transactional and service notifications, with a pre-flight check before anything is sent.
- Support-conversation design: greeting, triage, answers from approved facts, and a defined **escalation policy** to a human.
- CRM handoff and status-update flows (order, delivery, appointment), plus delivery-report reading.

## Try it
- "Draft three order-status templates and the preflight check for each."
- "Design the support flow for 'where is my order?', with an escalation rule."
- "Review this campaign plan: is every recipient opted in?"

## You bring
Your customer-service policy, approved facts (hours, shipping, returns), and the official WhatsApp Business Platform tooling exposed by your host.

## Boundaries
Instructions and templates. No credentials or connector included. No unsolicited bulk messages, purchased lists, evasion of limits, or unofficial clients. It does not manage social feeds or video.

Install: see the suite README, or `python scripts/install_skills.py --skill whatsapp-agent`.
