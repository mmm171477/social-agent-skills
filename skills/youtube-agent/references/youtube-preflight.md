# YouTube upload preflight

- [ ] Correct channel identity and authorization
- [ ] Source video and thumbnail rights confirmed
- [ ] Current YouTube format/duration/file constraints checked
- [ ] Title and thumbnail promise are aligned and truthful
- [ ] Description, links, credits, disclosure and chapters verified
- [ ] Captions/subtitles reviewed
- [ ] Audience, age restriction, paid promotion and visibility fields reviewed
- [ ] Exact schedule and time zone confirmed
- [ ] Human approval recorded
- [ ] Provider status checked after upload
