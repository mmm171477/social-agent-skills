# youtube-agent — packaging and publishing discipline for YouTube

For channel owners, editors and agencies who want every video to leave the edit bay with a title, thumbnail brief, description, chapters and captions that were *decided*, not improvised.

## What it gives you
- A **packaging-first** workflow: promise, audience, title/thumbnail pair, then the script, so the video earns its click.
- Long-form and Shorts planning, including how to cut a Short from a long video without a cold start.
- Title and thumbnail A/B briefs, chapter outlines, description structure, caption and translation checklists.
- A pre-flight check before any upload or metadata change, and an analytics reading guide that separates impressions, click-through and retention.

## Try it
- "Package this 14-minute script: three title/thumbnail pairs, chapters, description."
- "Turn this episode into five Shorts concepts with a first-line hook for each."
- "Review last month's analytics export and tell me which video to fix first and why."

## You bring
The channel context (topic, audience, tone), the script or transcript, and, only if you want it to publish, an official authorized YouTube tool exposed by your host.

## Boundaries
Instructions and checklists only. No credentials, no connector, no guaranteed views. It will not buy engagement or invent metrics. Details: `references/` and `examples/`.

Install: see the suite README, or `python scripts/install_skills.py --skill youtube-agent`.
