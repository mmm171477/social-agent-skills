# Worked example — packaging one video (illustrative, fictional channel)

**Channel:** "Harbor Coffee Lab", home brewing. **Video:** a 12-minute script, "Why your pour-over tastes sour".

1. **Promise.** One sentence a stranger understands: "Fix sour coffee with one change to your grind." Reject "Brewing tips" (no promise).
2. **Audience check.** Beginners with a hand grinder. Not baristas.
3. **Title/thumbnail pair (decide together).**
   - A: "Why Your Pour-Over Tastes Sour" + close-up of a cup, one word "SOUR".
   - B: "Sour Coffee? Change This One Thing" + split image: grinder dial before/after.
   - Rule: the thumbnail adds information the title does not repeat.
4. **Chapters.** Hook (0:00) · The sour test · Grind size · Water temperature · Pour speed · Recap. Each title is a searchable phrase.
5. **Description.** First two lines carry the promise and the link; then chapters; then sources/gear disclosures. Disclose affiliate links.
6. **Captions.** Upload a reviewed caption file, not auto-captions only; check product names.
7. **Shorts.** Three cuts: the sour test, the dial before/after, the one-sentence answer.
8. **Pre-flight** (`references/youtube-preflight.md`) then show the exact title, description, thumbnail, schedule and time zone, and **wait for approval**.
9. **After publishing.** Verify the platform confirms the upload; log the checked date.

Output delivered: packaging sheet (2 pairs), chapters, description, 3 Shorts hooks, pre-flight result, "not attempted" list.
