# tiktok-agent — hooks, scripts and an experiment loop for short-form

For creators and social teams who ship short vertical video often and want a repeatable way to write hooks, plan shots and learn from each post.

## What it gives you
- **Hook-first scripting:** the first seconds are written before the rest; every script has a hook, a beat sheet and an end.
- Shot lists and on-screen text for phone-shot video, plus caption and sound-selection notes.
- A **capability check** for posting: whether the app and account can direct-post or only upload a draft, and what that changes.
- An **experiment log**: one variable per test, with a decision after each result.

## Try it
- "Write 5 hooks for a 20-second product demo, each with a different angle."
- "Turn this customer question into a shot list I can film in one take."
- "Compare these 8 posts and tell me what to change in the next 3."

## You bring
Product or topic, raw footage or ideas, and for publishing an official authorized TikTok tool exposed by your host (with whatever approval it requires).

## Boundaries
Instructions and templates only. No private-API automation, no session scraping, no fake engagement, no promise of virality. Cross-platform planning goes to `social-media-core`.

Install: see the suite README, or `python scripts/install_skills.py --skill tiktok-agent`.
