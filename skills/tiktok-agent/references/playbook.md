# TikTok playbook

## 1. Write the hook first
The first seconds decide everything. Draft at least five hooks in different angles (mistake, result, question, challenge, story) and pick one before writing the rest. A good hook names a specific viewer and a specific payoff.

## 2. Beat sheet
Hook → problem shown → one change → result shown → one-line call to action. One idea per video. Cut anything that does not move a beat.

## 3. Native look
Phone-shot, vertical, natural voice, captions on screen, quick pacing. Polish matters less than clarity. Do not reuse a watermarked clip from another platform.

## 4. Sound and rights
Use audio only where the account type and use are licensed for it. When unsure, use original audio or mark the item for rights review.

## 5. Posting paths
Direct post and upload-as-draft differ in what the account must be approved for and what the creator must still do in the app. Verify with the capability check; never assume.

## 6. Experiment loop
| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis | "Hook B beats hook A for first-time buyers" |
| Change | One variable only |
| Window | Fixed before posting |
| Read | Watch-through and shares first, views second |
| Decision | Keep, kill or iterate, with one sentence why |

Small samples are hints; say so.

## 7. Never
Use unofficial automation or scraped sessions, fake engagement, invent analytics, or claim a post is live without confirmation.
