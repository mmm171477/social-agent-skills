# instagram-agent — native Instagram content with a publish-safety gate

For brands, creators and agencies running Instagram professional accounts who want Reels, carousels and Stories built for the platform, and a hard stop before anything goes live.

## What it gives you
- **Format selection:** Reel, carousel, Story or feed post, chosen from the goal, not habit.
- Reel briefs (first-frame hook, mobile-safe framing, on-screen text), carousel sequencing (cover, payoff slide, save-worthy last slide), Story sequences.
- Caption variants, alt text and hashtag strategy, plus rights checks for audio and creator permissions.
- Media validation and a publish checklist, and community-reply drafts routed to a human for anything sensitive.

## Try it
- "Plan a 7-slide carousel from this blog post; the last slide should be worth saving."
- "Write three Reel hooks for this product demo and a caption for each."
- "Draft replies to these 12 comments; flag any that need a human."

## You bring
Brand voice, assets, and the account context. For publishing you also need an official authorized Instagram integration exposed by your host.

## Boundaries
Instructions and checklists. No credentials or connector included, no guaranteed reach. Personal-account automation, scraping and engagement manipulation are out of scope.

Install: see the suite README, or `python scripts/install_skills.py --skill instagram-agent`.
