# Instagram playbook

## 1. Choose the format from the goal
| Goal | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Reach new people | Reel | built for discovery |
| Teach / be saved | Carousel | reference value, slow read |
| Keep existing followers warm | Stories | low-friction, sequential |
| Announce or show proof | Feed image or short carousel | permanent and scannable |

## 2. Reel checklist
- First frame states the problem or payoff; no logo animation first.
- One idea per Reel. If it needs two, make two.
- On-screen text readable without sound and inside the safe area.
- Ending delivers the payoff; do not trail off.
- Audio rights cleared for the account type.

## 3. Carousel structure
Cover (promise) → one point per slide → payoff slide → save-worthy summary → single call to action. Keep a consistent layout so swiping feels continuous.

## 4. Caption
Lead with the first line people see before truncation; put the call to action in the line, not buried. Provide a short and a story-led variant. A few specific hashtags beat many generic ones.

## 5. Community operations
- Reply to questions fast with specifics; thank, do not template.
- Draft only. Route complaints, legal threats, safety reports, payment issues and security incidents to a human.
- Never respond to comments or messages unless the exact permission and policy allow it.

## 6. Measurement
Read by format: reach and plays for Reels, saves and shares for carousels, replies and taps for Stories. State the time window. Do not compare formats on one metric.

## 7. Never
Automate a personal account, scrape, extract session cookies, fake engagement, or report a post as published before the provider confirms it.
