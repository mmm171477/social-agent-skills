# Worked example — blog post to carousel plus Reel (illustrative, fictional brand)

**Brand:** "Harbor Coffee Lab". **Source:** a blog post, "5 mistakes that make pour-over sour".

1. **Pick the format.** Goal = saves and shares from beginners → carousel (teaches, reference value) plus one Reel (reach).
2. **Carousel (7 slides).**
   - Slide 1 cover: the promise in 6 words, large text, one image.
   - Slides 2–6: one mistake per slide: bold claim, one line of why, one line of fix.
   - Slide 7: the checklist worth saving, plus a single call to action.
   - Add alt text per slide; keep text inside a safe margin for the phone crop.
3. **Reel (single idea).** First frame shows the problem (a sour cup reaction). Text overlay states the fix. End on the result. Use cleared audio only.
4. **Captions.** Two variants: short and story-led. Hashtags: few and specific, no stuffing.
5. **Checks.** Product claims, tags and mentions, audio rights, any branded-content disclosure, media order and crop.
6. **Approval gate.** Show account, exact slide order, captions, tags, post type, scheduled time and time zone. **Stop for approval.**
7. **After publishing.** Confirm the provider returned a media ID and a final status; if a call timed out, reconcile before retrying to avoid a duplicate post.
8. **Review in 7 days.** Saves and shares per format, not follower count; note one change for next time.

Output delivered: carousel script, Reel brief, caption variants, checklist result, approval summary, "not attempted" list.
